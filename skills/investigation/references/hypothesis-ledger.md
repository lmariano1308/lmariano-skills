# Hypothesis ledger

Keep three to five ranked explanations. Update rather than silently replacing them.

| Rank | Hypothesis | Why plausible | Falsifiable prediction | One-variable probe | Observation | Status |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Concise causal claim | Existing evidence | What must be observed if true | Smallest discriminating experiment | Evidence after execution | hypothesis / eliminated / probable / confirmed |

Rules:

- Make each hypothesis explain the exact symptom and expected-versus-observed gap.
- Reject statements that cannot predict a different observation.
- Run one probe at a time and preserve negative results.
- Re-rank when new evidence changes likelihood.
- Record contradictions and unknowns explicitly.
- After three low-information attempts, stop and review premises or design.
